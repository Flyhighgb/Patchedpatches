# Long-tail keyword research

Method (November 2026): no keyword tool was available, so competition was judged from what ranks today. A search counts as low competition when the top results are forum threads, iFixit community guides, Instructables, old blog posts or a single brand page, rather than big publishers with dedicated, up-to-date guides. Search volumes are not known; check them in Google Search Console once the guides are indexed.
