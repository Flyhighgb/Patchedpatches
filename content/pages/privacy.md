**Last updated: 19 November 2026**

Patched Patches is a free website of guides to patching and mending clothes, run from the UK. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it. We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name or contact details unless you email us.

## Cookies and your choices

When you first visit, you may see a message asking whether you agree to cookies being used for advertising. It's provided by Google, a certified consent management platform. You can say yes or no, and the site works the same either way. You can change your mind at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.

## Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which helps keep the guides free.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites.
- If you don't agree to personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use cookies only for things like limiting how often you see an ad and preventing fraud.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), and opt out of many other vendors' personalised ads at [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/uk/).
- You can read [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites and doesn't build a profile of you.

## Affiliate links

Some links go to retailers such as Amazon. If you click one, that retailer may set its own cookies so it can credit us if you buy something. Their privacy policies apply on their sites. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Our tools

The [patch layout tool](/patch-layout-tool/) runs entirely in your browser. It saves your layout in your browser's local storage so it's still there next time; nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/). To make a request, [email us](contact:email).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
