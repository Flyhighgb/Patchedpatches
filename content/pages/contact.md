We'd love to hear from you, whether you've spotted a mistake, have a mending question a guide doesn't answer, or want to show us a repair you're proud of.

The best way to reach us is to [email us](contact:email). We read everything and try to reply within a few working days.

## Good things to include

- **A correction?** The link to the guide and what needs fixing. We update guides and change their "Last reviewed" date.
- **A question?** What the garment is made of (check the care label) and a photo if you can.
- **A partnership or press enquiry?** Who you are and what you have in mind. We don't accept payment for recommendations; see [how we write our guides](/about/#how-we-write-our-guides).
- **A privacy request?** See our [privacy and cookies page](/privacy/) for what we hold, which is usually nothing.
