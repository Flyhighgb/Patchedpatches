## What's inside

- **Templates** for a denim jacket (front and back), a backpack, a cap and jeans, each with a patch list and tips.
- **An actual-size patch guide**, from 2.5 cm to 10 cm, with a ruler to check your printer.
- **Four layouts that always work**, from one classic chest patch to a full scatter.
- **A sketch page** with a 5 mm dotted grid.
- **A patch log** to track each patch, its size, backing and where it's going.
- **An iron, sew or glue cheat sheet**: what the iron symbols on care labels mean, and the best method for each fabric.
- **A shopping list and notes page.**

## Good to know

- It's a 12-page A4 PDF. Download it straight after you buy, and print as many copies as you like for your own use.
- Print at 100% or "Actual size" so the size guide measures true.
- New to patches? Our free guides cover [ironing on](/guides/how-to-iron-on-a-patch/), [sewing on](/guides/how-to-sew-on-a-patch-by-hand/) and [washing](/guides/how-to-wash-clothes-with-patches/).
