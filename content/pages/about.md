Patched Patches is a library of practical, UK-focused guides to patches: ironing them on, sewing them on, gluing them, washing them, taking them off again, and choosing the kit that makes it easy.

Every guide follows the same shape:

- **The quick answer first**, in plain English.
- **Numbered steps** you can follow at the ironing board.
- **What to avoid**, including the fabrics that can't take heat.

## How we write our guides

- **Based on manufacturer instructions and established sewing practice.** Where a patch, glue or iron comes with its own instructions, those come first, and our guides say so.
- **Fabric-safe.** Every guide that involves heat tells you how to check the care label first.
- **Honest about products.** We describe product types and features, and name popular, widely recommended examples. We never claim to have tested something we haven't, and we never invent reviews, ratings or prices.
- **Reviewed and updated.** Each guide shows the date it was last reviewed.
- **Independent.** Some links may earn us a commission (see our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)). That never decides what we recommend.
