Patched Patches is free to read. To keep it that way, some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

We only link to products that fit the advice in the guide. Commissions never decide what we recommend, and where the best answer is a needle and thread you already own, the guide will say so.

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

We also earn commission from some UK retailers through the Awin affiliate network, and we sell our own printable patch planner.
