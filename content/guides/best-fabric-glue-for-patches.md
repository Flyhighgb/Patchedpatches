Fabric glue is the answer when you can't iron and don't want to sew: on nylon and polyester jackets, caps, shoes, bags and anything heat-sensitive. A good permanent textile glue stays flexible and survives washing. The catch is time: most need hours to set and a day or more to cure fully before the first wash.

## What to look for

- **Permanent and washable.** Check the label says it's washable once cured. "Temporary" or "basting" glues are for holding fabric while you sew, not for keeping a patch on.
- **Dries flexible and clear.** A stiff glue cracks when the fabric bends, and the patch starts to lift.
- **Suits your fabric.** Some glues are made for synthetics, leather or outdoor gear. Read what the maker says it bonds.
- **A fine nozzle.** Makes it easy to put a thin, even layer right up to the patch edge without it oozing out.
- **Cure time.** Plan for the full cure time on the label before washing.

## How to glue on a patch

1. **Wash and dry the garment** to remove finishes that stop glue sticking.
2. **Put a piece of card or baking paper inside the garment** so glue can't soak through to the other side.
3. **Apply a thin, even layer** to the back of the patch, keeping about 2 to 3 mm (⅛ in) from the edge so it doesn't ooze out. Add a thin line around the edge itself.
4. **Press the patch in place** and smooth from the centre outwards.
5. **Weigh it down** with a book (with baking paper between) while it sets, for as long as the instructions say.
6. **Let it cure fully** before washing, usually at least 24 hours, sometimes longer. Check the label.

## What to avoid

- **Superglue and hot glue.** They set hard, crack when the fabric bends, and can leave stains or melt synthetics.
- **Too much glue.** It soaks through, stiffens the fabric and shows round the edges.
- **Washing too soon.** Uncured glue washes out.
- **Solvent glues in a closed room.** Open a window and keep them away from children.

## Common questions

### Is fabric glue as strong as sewing?
Not quite. A good permanent textile glue holds well through normal wear and washing, but stitching is still the most secure. For heavy-use items, glue and add a few stitches. See [how to sew on a patch by hand](/guides/how-to-sew-on-a-patch-by-hand/).

### Can you glue a patch onto a polyester or nylon jacket?
Yes. That's one of the best uses for fabric glue, because those fabrics often can't take an iron. Choose a glue that says it bonds synthetics.

### How long does fabric glue take to dry?
It varies by brand. Many set in a few hours and cure fully in 24 to 72 hours. Follow the label and wait for the full cure before washing.

## The short version

Choose a permanent, washable textile glue that dries flexible, apply a thin layer with card inside the garment, weigh it down and wait for the full cure before washing. Avoid superglue and hot glue. For the toughest hold, glue and stitch.
