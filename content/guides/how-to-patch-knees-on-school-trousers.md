Holes in the knees of school trousers are best fixed from the inside, so the repair doesn't show. Iron a patch behind the hole, glue side to the trousers, then stitch around the hole from the outside so it can't peel. If the hole is big or the school doesn't mind, a neat matching patch on the outside is the toughest fix. Both take 20 minutes and should see out the term.

## Check the trousers first

- **The care label.** Most school trousers are polyester or a polyester and viscose blend, which can't take a very hot iron. Use the temperature on the label and always iron through a pressing cloth or baking paper.
- **Worn or holed?** If the fabric is only thin and shiny, patch it now from the inside before it becomes a hole. It's much easier.
- **The size of the hole.** Small holes and thin patches suit an inside patch. Big rips or frayed edges need a patch big enough to reach good fabric all round, at least 2 cm (1 in) past the damage.
- **School rules.** Some schools are fine with outside patches; some uniform policies aren't. An inside patch is invisible from a distance either way.

## What you need

- **Iron-on mending patches** in black, grey or navy. [Iron-on school trouser patches](amazon:iron on mending patches black school trousers) are sold in packs and are made for this. Choose a twill or polyester patch, not a stiff denim one.
- **An iron**, a pressing cloth or baking paper, and a firm ironing surface.
- **A needle and thread** in a matching colour, for stitching round the edges.
- **Small, sharp scissors** to trim frayed threads.

## How to patch the knee from the inside

1. **Turn the trousers inside out** and trim any long frayed threads round the hole.
2. **Cut the patch** so it's at least 2 cm bigger than the hole on every side, and round off the corners. Rounded corners peel far less than square ones.
3. **Slide a piece of card** or a magazine inside the leg, so you don't fuse the front to the back.
4. **Lay the patch glue side down** over the hole on the inside, cover with a pressing cloth or baking paper and press firmly for the time on the packet, without sliding the iron.
5. **Let it cool**, then turn the trousers right side out.
6. **Press from the outside** through the cloth, around the hole, so the edges of the hole stick to the patch.
7. **Stitch round the hole** with small stitches through the trousers and the patch, using matching thread. This is what stops it lifting in the wash.

## Patching from the outside

For bigger rips, cut a patch from matching fabric, an old pair of trousers, or an iron-on patch, big enough to cover the damage with a good margin. Iron or pin it on the outside, then sew round the edge with small whip stitches or a neat running stitch about 3 mm in from the edge. Patching both knees makes it look deliberate. | Iron-on patch inside only | Barely | A few weeks to months | A quick fix |
| Sewn patch outside | Yes | Longest | Big rips, play trousers |
| Fabric glue patch | Barely | Varies | Fabrics that can't take heat |
| Fusible hemming web and fabric | Barely | A few months | No iron-on patches to hand |

## Make the repair last

- **Wait 24 hours** before washing an iron-on patch.
- **Wash inside out** at 30 or 40°C, and line dry if you can. Tumble dryers soften the glue.
- **Patch early.** Iron a patch inside both knees of new trousers before they wear through, and they'll last much longer.

## Don't

- **Don't use a hot iron** on polyester trousers without a cloth. It leaves a shiny mark or melts the fabric.
- **Don't forget the card inside the leg.**
- **Don't rely on glue alone** for a knee that bends all day. Add stitches.
- **Don't use a patch** only just bigger than the hole. It'll tear round the edge.

## Common questions

### Do iron-on patches work on school trousers?
Yes, especially on the inside, but on their own they often peel at the corners after a few washes because knees flex so much. A row of stitches round the hole makes them far more durable.

### What iron setting should I use for school trouser patches?
Use the setting on the trousers' care label, which is usually low to medium for polyester, and press for longer through a pressing cloth. Check the patch packet too and go with the lower of the two temperatures.

### Can you patch school trousers without sewing?
Yes. An iron-on patch on the inside will hold for a while on its own, and fabric glue works on trousers that can't be ironed. For something that lasts, add a few stitches when you get the chance.

### How do you stop school trousers wearing out at the knees?
Iron a thin patch inside each knee while they're new, and wash them inside out. Kneeling on playground tarmac will still win eventually, but they'll last noticeably longer.

## The short version

Turn the trousers inside out, iron a rounded patch over the hole with card inside the leg, then stitch round the hole from the outside so it can't peel. Use the care label temperature and a pressing cloth, and wait a day before washing.
