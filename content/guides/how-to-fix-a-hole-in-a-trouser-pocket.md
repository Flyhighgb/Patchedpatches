A hole in a trouser pocket is almost always in the pocket bag, the thin lining fabric inside, usually along the bottom seam where coins and keys rub. Turn the pocket inside out and either backstitch the seam closed a little above the hole, or iron or sew a patch over it. If the lining has gone thin all over, it's worth replacing the pocket bag. All three are easy by hand.

## Find out where the hole is

- **Along the bottom seam.** The most common hole. Stitch the seam closed above it.
- **In the lining fabric itself.** Patch it.
- **Lots of thin areas**, or the lining is shredding: replace the pocket bag, or the whole lower half of it.
- **In a coat pocket.** The lining is often sewn in, so you may need to open a lining seam to reach it. See below.

## What you need

- **Polyester thread** to match the lining, a hand-sewing needle and scissors.
- **Iron-on mending fabric or pocket lining fabric** for patches. [Iron-on mending fabric](amazon:iron on mending fabric patch) is quick; a scrap of cotton works for a sewn patch.
- **An iron** and a pressing cloth, if you're using an iron-on patch.

## Fix 1: sew the bottom seam closed

1. **Turn the pocket inside out** and pull it out of the trousers.
2. **Trim any frayed threads**, and turn the hole edges under if the fabric is ragged.
3. **Backstitch a new seam** about 1 cm (½ in) above the hole, starting and finishing 2 cm past it in the good fabric. Small stitches, about 3 mm.
4. **Sew a second line** a few millimetres below the first, for strength.
5. **Trim off the ragged edge**, leaving at least 5 mm below the stitching.
6. **Push the pocket back in**. It'll be a little shallower, which is rarely noticeable.

## Fix 2: patch the hole

1. **Turn the pocket inside out.**
2. **Cut a patch** at least 2 cm bigger than the hole all round, with rounded corners.
3. **For iron-on**, press it over the hole on the inside of the pocket through a pressing cloth, using the care label temperature. Then press the other side.
4. **For a sewn patch**, pin it over the hole and stitch around the edge with small whip stitches, then stitch around the hole through both layers.
5. **Push the pocket back** and press.

## Fix 3: replace the pocket bag

If the lining is worn through in several places, unpick it where it joins the trousers (usually along the top and sides of the front pocket opening), use it as a template, cut a new one from strong cotton pocketing or a cotton shirt, and stitch it back on along the old lines. This is the most work, but it's the permanent fix.

## Coat pockets

Coat pockets are usually hidden behind the lining. Find a seam in the lining near the pocket, or the opening many coats have in the bottom of the lining, unpick a few centimetres and reach in to sew or patch the pocket. Close the lining afterwards with [ladder stitch](/guides/how-to-fix-a-ripped-seam/).

## Fixes compared

| Problem | Fix | Time | What to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hole at the bottom seam | Sew a new seam above it | 10 minutes | Pocket gets slightly shallower |
| Hole in the fabric | Iron-on or sewn patch | 15 minutes | Stitch round iron-on patches to stop peeling |
| Lining thin everywhere | Replace the pocket bag | 1 hour | Use the old one as a pattern |
| Coat pocket | Open lining seam, then sew or patch | 30 minutes | Close the lining with ladder stitch |
| No sewing kit | Fabric glue or iron-on patch | 10 minutes | A temporary fix |

## Don't

- **Don't use a stiff patch** in a pocket. It feels bulky against your leg.
- **Don't stitch the pocket to the trousers' front.** Keep it pulled clear while you work.
- **Don't iron synthetic linings hot.** Many melt easily; check the label and use a cloth.
- **Don't leave a tiny hole.** They only get bigger.

## Common questions

### Can you fix a pocket hole without sewing?
Yes, with an iron-on mending patch or fabric glue over the hole, applied from the inside of the pocket. It's fine for a while, but a few stitches round the edge make it last.

### Why do trouser pockets get holes?
Keys, coins and phones rub the thin lining fabric, especially along the bottom seam. Lightweight polyester linings wear through fastest.

### How do you fix a hole in a jeans pocket?
Front pockets on jeans have a cotton pocket bag, which you fix exactly the same way. Back pockets are part of the denim itself, so a hole there needs a denim patch; see [how to patch a hole in jeans](/guides/how-to-patch-a-hole-in-jeans/).

### What fabric is best for pocket lining?
Firm, tightly woven cotton, such as pocketing, twill or a good shirt cotton. It's stronger than thin polyester lining.

## The short version

Turn the pocket inside out and either backstitch a new seam just above the hole or patch over it. If the lining is worn everywhere, replace the pocket bag. For coat pockets, open a lining seam to reach the pocket, then close it with ladder stitch.
