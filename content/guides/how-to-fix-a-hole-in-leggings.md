To fix a hole in leggings, turn them inside out, pinch the hole closed and sew it with small zigzag-style stitches using polyester thread and a ballpoint needle, leaving a little slack so the repair stretches with the fabric. Split seams in the crotch or inner thigh are fixed the same way along the seam line. Avoid iron-on patches and tight straight stitches: stretch fabric snaps them.

## Check the leggings first

- **Is it a seam or the fabric?** Most leggings holes start at the seams, especially the crotch and inner thigh, where the stitching breaks. That's the easiest fix.
- **How big is it?** Pinholes and holes up to about 1 cm (½ in) can be stitched closed. Bigger holes in the fabric need a patch of stretch fabric behind them.
- **What are they made of?** Most leggings are nylon or polyester with elastane. They stretch a lot and don't like heat, so no hot irons and no iron-on patches.

## What you need

- **Polyester all-purpose thread**, which has some give. Cotton thread snaps. Match the colour, or go slightly darker.
- **A ballpoint or stretch needle**, which slips between the fibres instead of piercing them and causing ladders. [Ballpoint hand-sewing needles](amazon:ballpoint hand sewing needles stretch fabric) are cheap.
- **Pins**, small scissors and a little clear nail varnish or [fray stopper](amazon:fray stop liquid) for the edges of the hole.
- **A scrap of stretch fabric** (an old pair of leggings or a sports top) for bigger holes.

## How to fix a small hole

1. **Turn the leggings inside out** and lay them flat, gently stretched, over something round like a jam jar or a darning mushroom.
2. **Seal the edges** of the hole with a dab of fray stopper or clear nail varnish, and let it dry. This stops it running.
3. **Thread a single strand** of polyester thread about 40 cm (16 in) long, and knot it.
4. **Start 5 mm before the hole** in good fabric.
5. **Pinch the hole closed** and sew across it with small diagonal stitches, going back and forth in a zigzag, about 2 mm apart.
6. **Don't pull tight.** After every few stitches, gently stretch the fabric to let the thread settle with some slack.
7. **Carry on 5 mm past the hole** and finish with a few tiny stitches on top of each other.
8. **Turn them right side out** and stretch to check.

## How to fix a split crotch or inner seam

1. **Turn the leggings inside out** and find where the stitching has broken.
2. **Pin the two edges together** along the original seam line, slightly stretched.
3. **Sew along the seam** with a small backstitch, or a zigzag over the edge, starting 2 cm before the split in the old stitching and ending 2 cm after it.
4. **Stretch the seam every few stitches** so the thread has slack.
5. **For a big crotch split**, add a small diamond of stretch fabric behind it (a gusset) and stitch round it, so the strain is spread out.

## Methods compared

| Hole | Best fix | Lasts? | What to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split seam | Backstitch or zigzag along the seam | Yes | Overlap the good stitching |
| Pinhole or snag | Zigzag stitch over it | Yes | Seal the edges first |
| Hole up to 1 cm | Zigzag stitch closed | Usually | Don't pull tight |
| Bigger hole | Stretch fabric patch behind, stitched | Yes | Use stretch fabric only |
| Thin, see-through areas | Often not worth repairing | — | Fabric is worn out |

## Don't

- **Don't use iron-on patches.** They don't stretch and they peel, and heat damages elastane.
- **Don't use a straight running stitch** on its own. It snaps when you bend.
- **Don't use cotton thread.**
- **Don't pull the stitches tight.** Leave slack.
- **Don't tumble dry** them afterwards. Heat weakens elastane and the repair.

## Common questions

### Can you sew leggings back together by hand?
Yes. A hand zigzag or backstitch with polyester thread and a ballpoint needle works well, as long as you leave enough slack for the fabric to stretch.

### Why do leggings get holes in the inner thigh?
Thighs rubbing together wear the fabric and stress the seam, and repeated squats stretch the crotch seam. Fix seam splits early, before the fabric next to them tears.

### Can you use fabric glue on leggings?
Most fabric glues dry stiff and crack on stretch fabric. A flexible fabric glue can seal a pinhole, but stitching is better for anything that stretches.

### How do I stop a hole in leggings getting bigger?
Seal the edges with fray stopper or clear nail varnish straight away, then stitch it when you can.

## The short version

Turn the leggings inside out, seal the edges of the hole, and sew it closed with small zigzag stitches in polyester thread with a ballpoint needle, stretching as you go so the thread has slack. Fix split seams along the old seam line the same way. Skip iron-on patches.
